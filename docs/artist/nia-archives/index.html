Nia Archives

Nia Archives is a producer, DJ, singer and songwriter whose music brings jungle and drum and bass into conversation with soul, pop and diaristic songwriting. Born in Bradford and raised in Leeds, she emerged through the UK electronic underground before reaching a wider audience with a run of acclaimed EPs. Her debut album, Silence Is Loud, was shortlisted for the 2024 Mercury Prize. Emotional Junglist brought her back to the shortlist in 2026.
