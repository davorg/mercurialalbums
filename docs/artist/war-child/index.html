War Child

War Child is an international charity working to support children affected by conflict. Music has long been central to its fundraising, most famously through the 1995 album Help, which brought together leading artists in aid of the charity. War Child Records continued that collaborative tradition with Help(2), shortlisted for the Mercury Prize in 2026. The artist credit represents the charity and the many musicians who contributed to the project.
