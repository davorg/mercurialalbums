Dave

Dave is a South London rapper, songwriter, musician and producer known for detailed storytelling and socially observant writing. His early EPs and singles established him as one of the leading voices in British rap. His debut album, Psychodrama, won the 2019 Mercury Prize. He followed it with We're All Alone in This Together in 2021, and returned to the shortlist in 2026 with The Boy Who Played the Harp.
