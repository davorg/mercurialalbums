JADE

JADE is the solo name of English singer and songwriter Jade Thirlwall. She first became widely known as a member of Little Mix, the chart-topping group formed on The X Factor in 2011. After Little Mix began a hiatus, JADE launched her solo career with Angel of My Dreams in 2024. Her debut album, That's Showbiz Baby!, received her first Mercury Prize nomination in 2026.
