Raye

Raye is a South London singer, songwriter and producer whose music moves between pop, R&B, jazz and dance music. After years of writing and recording within the major-label system, she became independent and released her debut album on her own terms. My 21st Century Blues was shortlisted for the 2023 Mercury Prize and established Raye as a major solo artist. She returned to the shortlist in 2026 with This Music May Contain Hope.
